# Font provenance and software dependencies

## Included font: Jigmo — CC0 1.0

The **font files** are released under CC0 1.0 by the author on the [official Jigmo site](https://kamichikoichi.github.io/jigmo/). The distribution archive also contains the CC0 legal text. This project includes only `Jigmo.ttf`, unmodified, plus a Unity-generated SDF asset; it does not include the Jigmo font-generation tools (which have a separate MIT license).

- Font and baked asset: `Assets/Demo/Fonts/jigmo/`.
- Original distribution README, CC0 text and acknowledgements are retained voluntarily for provenance. They are not a notice-retention condition under CC0.
- Exact archive URL, extracted-file SHA-256 hashes and official repository blob verification: [FONT-SOURCES.json](FONT-SOURCES.json).
- The website's displayed checksum is incomplete and differs from the current archive. We therefore compared the downloaded archive's Git blob hash to the official author's GitHub repository; these match. We do not claim its displayed SHA-256 passed.

CC0 has no attribution requirement and does not require seeking individual commercial-use permission. It does not provide warranties of title or non-infringement. No promise of immunity from claims is made.

No other font binaries are bundled under Assets. Previous third-party font assets and the standard TMP font have been removed; all demo text and TMP's default font point to Jigmo.

## Unity / TextMeshPro resources

The remaining TMP Essential Resources provide standard shaders, settings, styles and sprite resources. They and packages resolved through `Packages/manifest.json` retain their own terms. They are not relicensed under CC0 or this repository's MIT license. Package caches are not distributed as repository content.

The no-attribution font choice does not remove the license requirements of Unity, TextMeshPro or the project's MIT-licensed code.
